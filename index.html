What this first version can — and cannot — do

Can: estimate visible body landmarks, draw a skeleton overlay, and calculate approximate elbow/knee angles for the current video frame.

Cannot yet: reliably identify every cricket shot, track the bat or ball, judge timing/contact, or promise professional coaching accuracy. These angles are 2D estimates and depend on camera angle and visibility. Treat notes as practice hints, not a definitive technique score.

Privacy: this page uses the video file you select in your browser. The pose model files are downloaded from a public CDN when analysis starts, so internet is required for the model. The video itself is not intentionally uploaded by this page. Use your own video or get permission before analysing someone else's clip.
